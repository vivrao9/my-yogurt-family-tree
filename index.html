Mapping my yogurt family tree

Last updated April 1, 2026

When tens of millions of people are crammed together for generations, they develop a culture. Bacteria is no different.

From thayir sadham to shrikhand, we Indians love our yogurt. Indeed, I love yogurt so much, I once finished a large tub of it in one night.

Over 7,000 years ago in present-day Turkey, someone's milk went bad and they decided to try it anyway. The work "yogurt" derives from the Old Turkish root yog, which means "to intensify" or "to condense." Ancient Greeks called it oxygala, which means “sour milk.” Most Indians call it dahi, which comes from the Sanskrit word dadhi, which is the land where rivers of milk and curd flowed.
